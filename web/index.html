Compass Economic Calendar

A free, machine-readable schedule of U.S. macro releases, FOMC events, Treasury auctions and CME equity-index futures roll dates. Built for futures traders. All timestamps UTC.

Subscribe

Full calendar (.ics)

Every tracked event. Add compass_calendar.ics as a subscribed calendar in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Refreshes weekly.

High impact only (.ics)

FOMC, CPI, jobs, PCE, advance GDP and quad witching — roughly half the events, none of the routine bill auctions. Subscribe to compass_calendar_high_impact.ics instead if you want this one always on.

JSON feed

calendar.json — the normalized source of truth. One event per entry with event_type, market_impact, source_url and a plain-English note.

What's in it

  • FOMC meetings, statements (2:00pm ET), press conferences (2:30pm ET), SEP/dot-plot releases and minutes
  • Employment Situation, CPI, PPI, GDP and PCE release dates (8:30am ET)
  • Treasury auctions and quarterly refunding announcements
  • /ES, /NQ, /MES, /MNQ roll dates, expirations, quad witching and monthly OPEX

Sources

  • Federal Reserve FOMC calendar
  • FRED API
  • TreasuryDirect
  • CME equity index roll dates (computed)
